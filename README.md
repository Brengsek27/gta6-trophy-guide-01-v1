Leonida Trophy Atlas is a complete GTA VI trophy guide and platinum tracker: all 55 official trophies with a written, sourced guide for each, missable warnings, a roadmap to the platinum, and a tracker that remembers your progress in your browser. It runs offline in a desktop browser; your progress never leaves your machine.
## Run it
```sh
node serve.ts
```
Then open http://localhost:8080, browse the guide, and tick trophies off as you earn them.
